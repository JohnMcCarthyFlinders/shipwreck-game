Shipwreck Sampling Game

Four sites. $5,000 each. One career on the line.

How it works

  1. Pick any site on the map. Each one has its own story and its own research question.
  2. Buy the sampling methods you think can answer the question. You have $5,000 per site and cannot afford everything.
  3. Lock in your plan to see what happens. Each site scores out of 100.
  4. Finish all four sites. Your total out of 400 decides your career.
